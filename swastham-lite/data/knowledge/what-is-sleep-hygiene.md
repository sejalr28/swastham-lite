# What Is Sleep Hygiene?

Sleep hygiene refers to a set of daily habits and environmental practices that support consistent, good-quality sleep. It is not a single rule but a collection of behaviors covering when you sleep, where you sleep, and what you do in the hours leading up to sleep.

The core idea is that sleep is influenced by more than just how tired you feel. Light exposure, meal timing, physical activity, stress levels, and the physical sleep environment all interact to affect how easily you fall asleep and how restorative that sleep is.

Good sleep hygiene practices are generally low-risk and can be adopted gradually. They are commonly recommended as a first step before considering other interventions, because they address common, modifiable contributors to poor sleep such as irregular schedules, excessive evening screen use, and stimulant intake late in the day.

Sleep hygiene guidance is general wellness information. It is not a substitute for a medical evaluation, and it does not replace the input of a licensed healthcare provider, especially for anyone experiencing persistent sleep difficulty, suspected sleep apnea, or symptoms of a sleep disorder.
