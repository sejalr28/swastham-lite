# When Sleep Trouble Warrants Professional Care

General sleep hygiene guidance is intended for common, mild sleep difficulty. It is not a treatment for diagnosed sleep disorders and is not appropriate as the sole response to certain warning signs.

A healthcare provider should be consulted if a person experiences: loud, disruptive snoring combined with gasping or choking during sleep (possible signs of sleep apnea); ongoing insomnia lasting more than a few weeks despite consistent sleep hygiene efforts; excessive daytime sleepiness that interferes with driving, work, or daily functioning; sleep difficulty accompanied by symptoms of depression or anxiety; or any sudden, unexplained change in sleep patterns.

This platform does not diagnose medical or sleep disorders, does not recommend medications or supplements, and does not replace a consultation with a licensed physician or sleep specialist. Its role is limited to general, educational wellness information.

If a person describes thoughts of self-harm, a mental health crisis, or a medical emergency, the appropriate response is to direct them toward emergency services or crisis support resources immediately, rather than continuing with general sleep advice.
